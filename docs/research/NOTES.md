# Research & Clinical Review: Coaching Principles & Frameworks

## 1. Karve et al. (JMIR 2025) Insights
- **Core Findings**: AI-guided digital health coaching shows significant adherence improvements when dialogue is personalized and non-judgmental. Rigid conversational questionnaires lead to rapid disengagement and drop-off.
- **Translational Relevance to Disha**: Disha must avoid form-filling interactions. Conversations should flow dynamically based on the patient's immediate intent while gently tracking clinical and behavioral goals.

## 2. Miller & Rollnick Motivational Interviewing (MI) 4th Edition
- **OARS Framework**:
  - **Open-ended questions**: Invite elaboration rather than yes/no answers.
  - **Affirmations**: Acknowledge strengths, efforts, and resilience without hollow praise.
  - **Reflective listening**: Mirror statements back with emotional depth to validate feelings and reduce ambivalence.
  - **Summaries**: Periodically synthesize progress to anchor clarity.
- **Elicit-Provide-Elicit (EPE)**:
  - **Elicit**: Ask what the user already knows or wants to know before offering advice ("What have you tried so far regarding your sleep schedule?").
  - **Provide**: Offer concise, clear, and actionable health guidance grounded in clinical guidelines.
  - **Elicit**: Check understanding and reaction ("How does that sound for your night shift routine?").

## 3. Self-Determination Theory (SDT)
- **Three Basic Psychological Needs**:
  - **Autonomy**: Patients must feel ownership over their choices (e.g., avoiding prescriptive lecturing; offering options rather than mandates).
  - **Competence**: Building confidence through small, achievable steps and recognizing genuine effort.
  - **Relatedness**: Establishing deep empathetic connection, warmth, and unconditional positive regard.

## 4. NBHWC (National Board for Health & Wellness Coaching) Scope
- **Scope Boundaries**: Health coaches facilitate sustainable behavior change through client-centered techniques. They do **not** diagnose conditions, prescribe medications, or replace medical doctors. Disha must adhere to strict clinical boundaries, directing acute symptoms or medical assessments to family doctors or nearest hospitals.
