Clinical Governance & Medical Disclaimer

Authoritative operational boundaries, ICMR-NIN stance, and DPDP Act 2023 compliance.

Important Medical Notice

Disha AI is an assistive digital health coach, NOT a physician, hospital, or emergency care provider. Information provided is for lifestyle, nutritional, and physical education purposes based strictly on doctor-prescribed care plans. In any medical emergency, call 112 immediately.

1. Clinical Architecture Doctrine

Disha AI implements a strict Thick Deterministic Spine with a Thin Probabilistic Leaf architecture:

  • Zero Unsupported Diagnoses: The system will never attempt to diagnose, triage acute conditions, or replace clinician judgment.
  • Zero Prescription Alteration: Medication names, dosages, and regimens cannot be altered or suggested.
  • Closed-Domain Nutritional Guidance: Dietary recommendations are strictly bounded to the patient's approved ICMR schedule.
  • Physical Exercise PEP Guardrails: Patients with active hypertension or cardiovascular restrictions are blocked from unapproved strenuous exertion.

2. DPDP Act 2023 Data Sovereignty

In accordance with Section 8 of the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023:

  • All personally identifiable health records (PHI) are scrubbed before reaching external LLM providers.
  • Every conversational turn is recorded in an immutable, chained SHA-256 Merkle audit ledger for total clinical transparency.

3. Emergency Response Coordination

Whenever red-flag clinical symptoms (chest tightness, severe breathlessness, stroke indicators, acute trauma) are detected, the conversational pipeline aborts generative speech instantly and instructs the user to contact emergency services at 112.
